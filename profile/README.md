# PDFMate eBook Converter Professional 1.1.1 Overview & Guide

**PDFMate eBook Converter Professional 1.1.1** is a dedicated eBook format conversion and library management utility developed by Anvsoft Inc. Built for digital readers and e-Reader owners, it converts purchased or personal eBooks from platforms like Kindle, Adobe Digital Editions, Kobo, and Nook into universal, DRM-free formats so they can be read across any smartphone, tablet, or e-Reader device.

## Key Features & Highlights in Version 1.1.1

* **Broad Format Compatibility:** Supports over 24 input formats (including KFX, AZW3, PRC, EPUB, MOBI, HTMLZ, and DOCX) and outputs directly to EPUB, MOBI, PDF, TXT, AZW3, and KFX.
* **Multi-Platform Library Support:** Imports and processes eBooks sourced from Kindle for PC, Adobe Digital Editions, Kobo Desktop, and Nook apps.
* **Auto-Device Detection:** Automatically scans and recognizes eBooks stored on connected hardware e-Readers (such as Kindle Paperwhite, Kindle Oasis, and Kobo Forma) via USB.
* **Batch Conversion Engine:** Processes entire eBook collections simultaneously while preserving original text layout, embedded fonts, and chapter structures.
* **Built-In Metadata Editor:** Allows users to inspect and edit essential book tags—including title, author, publisher, publication date, ISBN, and cover artwork—prior to exporting.

## Download & Setup Instructions

1. **Download Official Installer:** Obtain the official Windows setup executable (`pdfmate-ebook-converter.exe`) directly from the official [PDFMate Download Center](https://www.pdfmate.com/download.html).
2. **Run Setup Wizard:** Double-click the downloaded installer file and follow the on-screen wizard prompts to accept the license agreement and select your target directory.
3. **Import eBooks:** Launch the application and click **Add eBook** to browse local folders, or select connected apps (Kindle, Adobe, Kobo, Nook) from the left-hand source panel.
4. **Select Output Format & Metadata:** Choose your desired target format (EPUB, PDF, MOBI, etc.) from the bottom format dropdown, and click the **Edit** icon next to any title if you wish to modify metadata tags.
5. **Activate & Convert:** Click the **Key** icon in the top menu bar to register your official license email and registration code, then click **Convert** to process your queue.
